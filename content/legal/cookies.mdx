**Short version:** we don't use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

## What we store on your device

| Name | Type | Purpose | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `zl-preloaded` | Session storage | Shows the intro animation only once per visit | Until you close the tab |
| `zl-currency` | Local storage | Remembers whether you prefer ₹ or $ | Until you clear it |
| `zl-cookie-notice` | Local storage | Remembers that you've seen this notice | Until you clear it |
| `zl-saved-news` | Local storage | Your saved AI News stories when you're not signed in | Until you clear it |
| `zl-utm` | Local storage | The campaign that first brought you here, attached to an enquiry if you send one | 90 days |
| Supabase auth | Cookie / local storage | Keeps you signed in if you use magic-link sign-in | Session, refreshed while active |

## Analytics

We use privacy-friendly, cookieless analytics (Vercel Analytics or Plausible) to count page views and key actions in aggregate. They don't identify you or follow you across other sites.

## Third-party embeds

If you open the booking calendar (Calendly), that service may set its own cookies, governed by its own policy.

## Your choices

You can clear or block storage in your browser settings at any time. The site will still work, though some preferences won't be remembered.
