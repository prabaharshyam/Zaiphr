## Overview

Kōen (Japanese for "park") is a fictional specialty roaster in Bengaluru opening its first café and launching an online bean subscription. It needed a brand that felt calm and crafted, without the kraft-paper look every third-wave café already has.

## Positioning

We built everything around **a pause in the city**: ten minutes away from the noise, with coffee worth slowing down for. That idea sets the tone of voice (unhurried, warm, never precious), the photography (close, natural light, hands in frame) and even the shop (roasted on Tuesdays, shipped fresh, no endless SKUs).

## Identity

The mark is a circle split by a single path. It reads as a coffee bean and as a path through a park. The palette follows roast stages, from crema to deep roast, with a sunset orange to make the brand easy to spot on a shelf or a street. Fraunces gives the wordmark and headlines warmth, and DM Sans keeps menus and labels clear.

## Packaging

Three roasts, three colours, one system. Each pouch carries the origin, process, tasting notes and roast date, so the packaging does the barista's job on the shelf.

## Website

The site sells beans, explains the cafés and teaches people to brew. Product cards lead with tasting notes rather than jargon, and the subscription is two clicks from anywhere.

## Launch content

A launch kit of more than 40 assets: opening posts, reels covers, menu boards and a summer cold-brew campaign, all built on the same grid so the café team can make new ones in Figma.

## Projected impact

Design targets for a concept, not client results: a brand people recognise from across the street, a consistent look on every cup and screen, and a launch kit ready on day one.
