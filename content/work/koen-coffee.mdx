## The challenge

Kōen (Japanese for "park") is a fictional specialty roaster opening its first café. It needed a brand that felt calm and crafted without falling back on the same kraft-paper look as every other third-wave café.

## Our approach

We built the positioning around the idea of **a pause in the city**: a short break with good coffee. The mark is a circle split by a single path, which reads as both a park and a coffee bean. Colour comes from roasting stages, from green bean to deep roast, with a sunset orange accent.

## The solution

A complete identity system: wordmark and symbol, a type pairing, a colour palette, bag and cup packaging, café signage and a launch content kit with reels, posts and in-store menus. Every asset was built on a grid so the team can make new ones in Figma without a designer.

## Projected impact

Design targets, not client results: a recognisable shelf presence, a consistent look across every touchpoint, and a launch kit ready on day one.
