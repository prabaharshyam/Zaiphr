## Overview

Millions of people in India want to move into AI work, and almost all of them hit the same wall: thousands of videos and courses, no order to them, and no way to know what a specific job actually needs. Xplaind turns that mess into one map. You pick the job you want and it takes you there, one 20-minute topic at a time.

## The problem

The pattern is familiar to anyone who has tried: start a course, lose the thread, jump to a different video and start over. Beginners didn't know whether "AI Engineer" and "ML Engineer" were different jobs. Working professionals didn't have time to find out. And most learning sites ranked courses by who paid for placement.

So the platform had three jobs: make the destination concrete, make each step small enough to finish, and stay honest about what's worth your time.

## Start from the job, not the topic

Everything begins with a role. Nine roadmaps (AI Engineer, Generative AI and LLM Engineer, Forward Deployed Engineer, AI Product Manager, Agentic AI Engineer, AI Automation Specialist, AI Governance and Compliance, Machine Learning Engineer and MLOps Engineer) each show how much coding they need and how many lessons they take. Unsure? A 10-minute level test suggests one.

## Lessons sized to a commute

Each topic is a plain-English explainer of about 20 minutes, an example from Indian work life, a quick check, and the one course or video worth your time. Lessons are shared across roadmaps, so progress on one role counts towards another. Courses are picked on merit: no paid rankings and no ads.

## Practice and getting hired

Learning stops mattering if it doesn't get you hired, so the second half of the product is about proof:

- **Interview practice out loud.** 205 real AI interview questions, answered against the clock, with feedback on what you covered and what you missed.
- **Study from your own notes.** Upload class notes or a textbook and get a timed test or flashcards, written only from that material, with every answer pointing to the page it came from. Files are read on the device and not stored.
- **A plan for your week.** The next lessons fitted to the hours you actually have, with a Monday reminder.
- **Resume and jobs.** Fixes in order of importance, the keywords recruiters search for, and salary ranges and live openings for each AI role in India.

## Built for phones first

Xplaind is built for short gaps in the day, on whatever device is at hand, so every screen was designed at phone width first: roadmaps collapse into a vertical path, lessons read like an article, and practice tools work one-handed.

## Pricing that respects students

The core roadmaps, lessons and projects are free. Pro adds an AI tutor, interview coach and resume reviewer, sold as one-time passes (a 30-day pass and a placement-season pass) rather than an auto-renewing subscription, with UPI, cards and net banking, GST included and a 7-day refund.

## What shipped

A fast Next.js platform on Vercel with nine role roadmaps, 206 lessons, 205 interview questions, 774 hand-picked courses and videos, 23 guided projects, AI-powered study and career tools, and dark mode. It is live at xplaind.in.
