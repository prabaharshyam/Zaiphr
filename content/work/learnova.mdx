## Overview

Learnova is a fictional study app we designed as a showcase build for Class 9 to 12 students on the CBSE and ICSE boards. The idea came from a simple worry parents have about AI homework helpers: they hand over the answer, and the child learns nothing.

## Hints first, answers last

Learnova never opens with the solution. A student snaps a question and the app asks what they'd try first, then gives the smallest useful hint. Each step has a quick check before the next one appears. The full worked answer is available, but only after the student has had a go.

<Insight>Stuck on a problem? Get a hint, not the answer.</Insight>

## Doubts in your own language

Many students think in Hindi and write in English. Voice doubts work in either, or both mixed, and the explanation comes back the same way, with a diagram when it helps.

## Practice that adapts

Daily plans are sized to the time the student has, and questions get harder as mastery grows. Before exams, board-style mock papers with timers and marking schemes take over.

## A report parents can actually read

Every Sunday parents get a plain-language report on WhatsApp: time studied, topics mastered, where their child is stuck and one thing they can do to help this week.

## Projected impact

Design targets for a showcase build, not client results: students who reach the answer themselves more often, a habit of short daily study and parents who know how it's going without asking twice.
