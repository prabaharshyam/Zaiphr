## Overview

Pulse Athletic is a fictional boutique studio in Koramangala running strength, conditioning, mobility and HIIT classes. Its members love it. Its Instagram didn't show why: polished but forgettable posts, and ads that looked like every other gym's.

## The insight

People don't scroll past fitness content because it's ugly. They scroll past because it says nothing in the first three seconds. So every piece starts from a hook (a line someone would say out loud) and the edit is built backwards from it.

<Insight>You don't need motivation. You need a 6:30 slot.</Insight>

## The content engine

One shoot day a month with three coaches gives us the raw material for around 30 pieces. Clips go through an AI-assisted rough cut, captions in two languages and automatic reframing into 9:16, 4:5 and 1:1, then a human editor does the final pass. A shared calendar shows what's live, testing, in edit and drafted, with a hook bank ranked by hook rate.

## The creative system

A condensed display face, high-contrast black and white photography and one red. Hooks sit in the lower third where thumbs don't cover them, and every creative ends on one action: book a free class.

## The studio website

The ads point to a site that matches them: the schedule on the home page, spots left on each class and a free trial one tap away.

## Projected impact

Design targets for a concept, not client results: a steady rhythm of content that sounds like the studio, faster turnaround from shoot to post, and ads judged on trial bookings rather than likes.
