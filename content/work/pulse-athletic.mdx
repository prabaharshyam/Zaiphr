## The challenge

Pulse Athletic is a fictional strength studio with great coaches and almost no content. When it did post, the videos were long, quiet and ignored.

## Our approach

We built a repeatable content format system: **Coach's cue**, **30-day reset**, **Member wins** and **Myth vs. fact**. Each format has a fixed hook structure, caption style and end card. We batch-shoot one day a month and edit for the rest.

## The solution

An editing pipeline with AI-assisted transcription, captioning and cut-downs, so one shoot produces reels, stories and 4:5 ad creatives. Every asset is tagged by format and hook so paid spend goes to what earns attention.

## Projected impact

Design targets, not client results: a reliable posting rhythm, faster turnaround and a library of tested hooks to reuse in ads.
