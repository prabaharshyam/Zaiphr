## The challenge

Spice Route Kitchens is a fictional group of three restaurants serving dishes from eight Indian regions. Aggregators brought volume but took up to 30% of every order, and the website was a PDF menu.

## Our approach

We made the website the best place to order: richer than an aggregator listing, with each region's story and signature dishes. Direct ordering is rewarded with loyalty credits, and repeat customers can reorder in two taps.

## The solution

A Next.js site with region-led menus, table reservations and direct ordering connected to the POS. n8n automations print kitchen tickets (KOTs), alert managers when stock runs low, and send a WhatsApp review request an hour after delivery.

## Projected impact

Design targets, not client results: a growing share of orders moved to the commission-free channel, faster kitchen handoffs, and steadier review volume.
