## Overview

Spice Route Kitchens is a fictional restaurant group in Bengaluru and Chennai with one idea: eight regional kitchens under one roof, each run by a chef from that region. Its food is distinctive, but online it looked like every other listing on the delivery apps, and the apps took a large cut of every order.

## The goal

Give regulars a reason to order direct: a better experience and a better price. That meant a site that sells the story, a menu that's easier to browse than an app, and an ordering flow as quick as the apps people already know.

## Menus by region

The menu is organised by where the dish comes from (Tamil Nadu, Kerala, Delhi, Punjab and so on) with a line on each dish's origin. Food photography leads, and every card can be added to the order in one tap.

## Ordering and tracking

Direct orders are 10% cheaper than the apps every day, paid by UPI in one tap and tracked live from the pass to the door. Table bookings take 20 seconds and are confirmed on WhatsApp.

<Figure src="/work/spice-route-kitchens/mobile.jpg" alt="Three phones: the cinematic mobile homepage, the menu and the order tracking screen with UPI payment" caption="Mobile: home, menu and checkout." />

## Behind the pass

Every order, whether direct, dine-in or from an app, lands on one kitchen display, routed to the right regional station. The same system sends inventory alerts and a review request after delivery, and tracks the share of direct orders over time.

## Projected impact

Design targets for a concept, not client results: a growing share of orders placed direct, lower commission costs and regulars who come back through the restaurant's own channel.
