## Overview

Fynly is a fictional invoicing product we designed and specified as a showcase build: the kind of MVP we'd take from idea to app stores for a founder. Its users are freelancers and small agencies in India who are good at their work and bad at chasing money.

## The problem

Getting paid is the most awkward part of freelancing. Invoices go out as PDFs, GST is worked out by hand, and the follow-up is a WhatsApp message nobody wants to send. The result is weeks of unpaid work and a cash-flow surprise every month.

## What we designed

- **GST-ready invoices in a minute.** The tax is worked out from the client's state: CGST and SGST for the same state, IGST across states. HSN and SAC codes are remembered per client.
- **UPI on every invoice.** A pay link and QR code that open any UPI app, with payment marked automatically when it lands.
- **Reminders that sound like you.** Polite nudges before and after the due date, drafted by AI in the freelancer's tone and sent on WhatsApp or email with one tap.
- **A forecast you can trust.** Expected cash for the next 90 days, based on open invoices and each client's payment habits.

## Mobile app

The app is built for the moment between meetings: see who owes what, send a reminder, share a pay link. One React Native codebase covers iOS and Android, sharing logic with the web app.
