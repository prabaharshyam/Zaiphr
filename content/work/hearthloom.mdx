## Overview

Hearthloom is a fictional brand selling handmade ceramics, throws and planters from artisan studios across India. Its products look best in real homes, not on studio backdrops, which makes home-décor creators the perfect channel, if the programme is run well.

## The approach

Instead of one big influencer, we planned two waves of 24 to 26 micro-creators in the 10k to 80k follower band. They have audiences that trust them, homes that suit the products and rates a young brand can afford. Each one gets a seeding kit chosen for their space, a short brief and a personal discount code.

## The creator brief

Briefs are short and visual, and they're readable on a phone: what's in the kit and where it is, the deliverables, the due date and two things to avoid. Drafts are uploaded for a quick review before posting.

## One dashboard for the whole programme

Invites, acceptances, shipping, drafts, live posts, reach and code redemptions all sit in one place, so the brand team knows exactly where every kit and every post is. Usage rights are cleared up front, so the best creator content can run as ads.

## The storefront

We refreshed the store for the festive edit so the traffic from creators lands somewhere that feels like their posts: warm photography, makers' names on every product and a #HomeWithHearthloom gallery.

## Projected impact

Design targets for a concept, not client results: steady, believable content from homes like the customer's, every post traceable to sales, and a library of rights-cleared creative for paid ads.
