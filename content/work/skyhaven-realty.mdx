## Overview

Skyhaven Realty is a fictional Bengaluru developer launching a residential project in Whitefield. Its ads bring in hundreds of enquiries a week from Meta, Google, property portals and the website. The sales team can only call so many, and by the time they ring a lead from last night, a competitor already has.

## The challenge

Most property enquiries are window shopping. A few are buyers with a budget, a loan and a deadline. Sales teams spend their mornings calling everyone to find the few, while the serious buyers wait. The brief was to reply to every enquiry instantly, find the serious ones and put them in front of a relationship manager with a visit already booked.

## The conversation

Every ad and form leads to one WhatsApp thread. The assistant greets the buyer with the project and asks four things, as tap-to-answer buttons rather than typing: size, budget, timeline and how they're funding it. It answers common questions (price, possession date, distance to the metro) from the approved brochure and offers live site-visit slots from the sales calendar.

## Scoring and routing

Answers and intent signals feed a 0 to 100 score. Leads above 80 go to a relationship manager in real time with a written summary, so the first call starts with "I hear you're relocating from Pune" instead of "How can I help?". Lower scores get a gentle follow-up on day 1, 3 and 7.
