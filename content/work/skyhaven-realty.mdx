## The challenge

Skyhaven is a fictional mid-size developer selling 2 and 3 BHK apartments in Pune. Its Meta ads bring in hundreds of enquiries a week. Most are cold, a few are ready to buy, and the sales team spends its mornings calling everyone in the order they came in.

## Our approach

We designed a five-question qualification flow covering budget, configuration, timeline, financing and preferred location. It runs as a natural WhatsApp conversation rather than a form, and answers questions about amenities, possession dates and pricing from a verified project sheet.

## The solution

Lead-ad submissions trigger an n8n workflow that opens a WhatsApp conversation within seconds. Claude runs the dialogue, extracts structured answers and computes a lead score. Hot leads are pushed to the CRM with a summary and offered site-visit slots, warm leads enter a nurture sequence, and every lead gets a reply even at 2 AM.

## Projected impact

Design targets, not client results: every enquiry answered in under a minute, sales calls focused on the top fifth of leads, and a full audit trail of every conversation in the CRM.
